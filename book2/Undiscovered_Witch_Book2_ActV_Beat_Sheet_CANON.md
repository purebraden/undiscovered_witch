# Undiscovered Witch — Book 2  
## Act V Beat Sheet (Canonical, Annotated)

### Act V Function
Act V exists to convert suspicion into confirmation —  
not for Allison,  
but for the world observing her.

The failure is not dramatic.  
It is procedural.

Something that should have happened everywhere  
did not happen here.

---

## Beat 1 — Regional Disruption (Off-Page)

- Livestock illness emerges in surrounding towns.
- Same feed supplier.
- Same batch window.
- Losses confirmed.

This is treated as routine inevitability.

Alders Creek is assumed included.

---

## Beat 2 — Local Expectation (Allison POV)

- Adults speak in conditional terms: “when it hits here,” “after this passes.”
- Farmers prepare quietly.
- No panic — just readiness.

Allison absorbs this as background unease.

---

## Beat 3 — The Non-Event

- The expected illness window passes.
- No cattle fall ill.
- No response triggers.
- No official notice explains why.

This is not remarked on immediately.

The town waits for the bruise that never forms.

---

## Beat 4 — Comparison Pressure (Off-Page)

- Neighboring towns confirm losses.
- Insurance and mitigation activate.
- Supply chains document impact.

Alders Creek becomes an unspoken anomaly.

---

## Beat 5 — Confirmation Check (Off-Page, Implied)

- A verification is run.
- The outcome exists regionally.
- Alders Creek returns **no event state**.

Deviation becomes provable.

No announcement is made.

---

## Beat 6 — Evan’s House: The TV Is On (Allison POV)

- Allison arrives for an ordinary visit.
- The television is already on in the background.
- Regional news reports livestock illness in surrounding towns.
- Maps highlight affected areas.
- Alders Creek is visibly absent.

The omission is visible. It is not explained.

---

## Beat 7 — Adult Reaction

- Mara and Jonah react before Allison does.
- The volume is lowered.
- Conversation is redirected gently.

They acknowledge oddness without analysis.
They contain without explaining.

---

## Beat 8 — Allison’s Perception

- Allison notices the omission.
- She notices adult hesitation.
- She notices the careful redirection.

She does not interpret this causally.

Her takeaway:
*This mattered more than they’re saying.*

---

## Beat 9 — Relational Escalation (Annotated)

- Evan makes a small, conscious choice to remain with Allison despite subtle social friction.
- This choice is not explained or discussed.
- It registers as presence, not declaration.

**Canonical Guarantee:**  
Evan aligns with Allison once under quiet pressure.  
This choice is observed but not interrogated.

---

## Beat 10 — Repositioning Escalates

- Allison is subtly redirected away from farms and feed-adjacent spaces.
- Invitations change shape.
- Adults manage proximity without instruction.

Still polite.  
Still indirect.

---

## Beat 11 — Authority Softening

- Rules hesitate near Allison.
- Corrections are phrased conditionally.
- Authority avoids firm edges in her presence.

Not fear.  
Not reverence.

Assessment.

---

## Beat 12 — Peer Echo

- Friends mirror adult behavior unconsciously.
- Topics shift when Allison is present.
- She becomes a conversational boundary.

She is included — but buffered.

---

## Beat 13 — Act V End State (Locked)

By the end of Act V:

- Allison does not know what failed.
- The town cannot dismiss what didn’t happen.
- Adult authority has shifted posture.
- Evan has made a documented alignment choice.
- The confluence has confirmation.

Alders Creek is now a location where certainty failed.

Allison remains the center of observation.
