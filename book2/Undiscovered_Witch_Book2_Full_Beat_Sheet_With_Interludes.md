# SIDEBAR — YA ROMANCE ARC (ALLISON & EVAN)

**Function:**  
Ground Evan’s continued presence and loyalty in emotional connection rather than structural knowledge.

This arc runs *in parallel* to the Confluence pressure and explains why Evan remains close when no formal role compels him to do so.

---

## ACT I — Recognition (Chs 1–5)

- Evan notices Allison’s attunement to moments others miss.
- Shared quiet establishes mutual curiosity and emotional safety.
- Romance is potential, not promise.

**Romance Signal:**  
Comfort without expectation.

---

## ACT II — Trust (Chs 6–10)

- Evan acts as a buffer between Allison and social pressure.
- He respects her refusals and never reframes them as problems to solve.
- Small choices (walking her home, staying late, asking nothing) build trust.

**Romance Signal:**  
Allison associates Evan with choice, not pressure.

---

## ACT III — Vulnerability (Chs 11–15)

- Evan admits fear *for* Allison, not of her.
- Allison shares uncertainty she voices nowhere else.
- Physical closeness increases in quiet, YA-appropriate ways.

**Critical Beat:**  
Evan stays *after* Allison’s refusal, knowing it changes things.

**Romance Signal:**  
Chosen presence.

---

## ACT IV — Anchor (Chs 16–20)

- As the world compresses options, Evan expands presence.
- He adapts quietly to Allison’s changing internal weight.
- He notices shifts through her emotions, not external explanation.

**Romance Signal:**  
He does not move away when others do.

---

## ACT V — Commitment Without Labels (Chs 21–23)

- Evan chooses to stay without invoking destiny, structure, or obligation.
- He remains present through the cost Allison absorbs.
- The relationship stabilizes without being named.

**End State:**  
They are not labeled as a couple — but they are chosen.

---

**Series Note:**  
This romance mirrors the Confluence ethic:
- quiet
- non-coercive
- consent-forward
- presence over force
