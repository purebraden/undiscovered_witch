# Chapter 9.5 — Interlude: Signals

The English teacher reread the sentence twice before deleting it.

It wasn’t wrong. It just felt like it would create work that didn’t need to exist yet. He closed the grading portal and told himself he’d revisit it later. The moment passed without consequence.

In the counseling office, a note sat open on a screen longer than usual. The counselor adjusted the wording, softened it, then archived it without escalation. Nothing in the file required action. The system accepted that.

At the library desk, a routine entry was logged and left unflagged. The librarian noticed how quickly Allison corrected herself when a book slipped from her hands. Reflexive. Considerate. Easy to misread as confidence. She made no comment and moved on to the next task.

In the hallway, a staff member watched Allison step aside to let a group pass, even though there was room. The instinct registered as awareness, not avoidance. It seemed… functional. The observation ended there.

A teacher paused mid-sentence when Allison entered the room, then continued without remark. The adjustment felt polite, invisible. The class moved on.

None of these moments were shared.

They didn’t accumulate into concern.  
They didn’t justify follow-up.  

Each decision ended where it began.

Individually, they meant nothing.

Together, they formed a pattern no one was yet prepared to name.
