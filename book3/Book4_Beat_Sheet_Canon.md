# Undiscovered Witch — Book 4 Beat Sheet
## Integration: Becoming Useful

**Canon Status:** Draft → Lock on approval  
**Series Movement:** II — Integration  
**Series Function:** Infrastructure, awareness, political seeding  
**End State:** Increased responsibility without power escalation

---

## GOVERNING QUESTIONS

**Primary:**  
What does it cost to be useful inside a system that claims neutrality?

**Secondary (Introduced, Not Resolved):**  
What happens when a system encounters someone who can grow beyond its design?

---

## CORE BOOK 4 CANON RULES

- The institution is referred to exclusively as **the Confluence**
- Allison does **not** use, integrate, or resonate with foreign magic
- Allison **does** discover that her magic is expandable
- Expandability is framed as identity and obligation, not power
- Tomas accompanies Allison on all interworld activity
- Evan remains emotional anchor and consent advocate
- Political entanglements are seeded, not resolved
- Fairy World is the sole non-native world visited

---

## MAGIC PROGRESSION (BOOK 4 — LOCKED)

### Allison Learns
- Magic systems are structural, not exclusive
- Royal inheritance grants access, not limitation
- Her magic recognizes systems, not expressions
- Expansion is possible — and dangerous

### Allison Does NOT
- Actively use foreign magic
- Resonate with another system
- Integrate or retain foreign magic
- Perform multi-magic actions

Book 4 = awareness of the door, not opening it.

## CONFLUENCE CHARACTER INTRODUCTIONS (BOOK 4)

### Tomas — Tutor / Calibration Specialist
- Role: Magical tutor, calibration guide, consent monitor
- Function: Ensures recognition does not slide into activation
- Relationship to Allison: Teacher → witness → advocate
- Relationship to Evan: Mutual respect, shared concern
- Canon Rule: Allison does not approach thresholds without Tomas present

### Iria Vell — Senior Mediator
- Role: Cross-world diplomacy
- Temperament: Polished, exhausted, pragmatic
- Conflict Axis: Collective calm vs individual clarity

### Toren Ashwill — Systems Analyst (Non-Magical)
- Role: Outcome variance tracking
- Temperament: Curious, blunt
- Conflict Axis: Measurement vs doctrine

### Elder Ren Caldris — Infrastructure & Continuity
- Role: Oversees crossings as systems
- Temperament: Defensive, precise
- Conflict Axis: Control vs adaptation

### Lysa Merewen — Junior Liaison
- Role: Fairy assignment support
- Temperament: Idealistic, anxious
- Conflict Axis: Idealism vs institutional pressure

### Naelis of the Thread — Fairy World Envoy
- Role: Interface with Fairy Courts
- Nature: Layered, non-embodied
- Conflict Axis: Obligation vs autonomy

# CHAPTER-BY-CHAPTER BEAT SHEET

## ACT I — Assigned Belonging

**Chapters 1–6**

1. Formal reintegration under Confluence language
2. Tomas assigned as travel tutor
3. Introduction of Iria Vell and Elder Ren Caldris
4. Archivist references to adaptive lines
5. Allison senses her magic as unfinished
6. Evan notices institutional language shift

**Act I End:** Allison understands she is useful — and incomplete by design.

---

## ACT II — Recognition Without Permission

**Chapters 7–12**

7. Sanctioned travel to the Fairy World
8. Introduction of Naelis and Lysa Merewen
9. Allison perceives fairy magic structurally
10. Tomas halts observation due to strain
11. Toren detects outcome variance
12. Allison is asked not to explore further

**Act II End:** Allison learns she could become more — and why she shouldn’t.

---

## ACT III — Structural Fear

**Chapters 13–18**

13. Historical expandable case referenced
14. Elder debate: adaptation vs containment
15. Royal briefings quietly requested
16. Evan challenges the prohibition logic
17. Naelis frames fairy magic as entry, not acquisition
18. Tomas confirms expansion cannot be undone

**Act III End:** Expandability threatens systems, not safety.

---

## ACT IV — The Cost of Not Choosing

**Chapters 19–24**

19. Fairy imbalance could be solved through alignment
20. Silence becomes pressure
21. Allison nears alignment
22. Tomas asks if the moment is choosing her
23. Allison stops herself; backlash confirms truth
24. The Confluence records stability restored

**Act IV End:** Allison remains singular by choice.

---

## EPILOGUE — Awareness Is Irreversible

25. Allison records her realization privately
26. Tomas remains assigned
27. Evan stays
28. The Fairy World is classified high-risk for Allison

---

## BOOK 4 END STATE (LOCK)

- Allison is useful to the Confluence
- The Confluence is uneasy with her existence
- Expandability is canon
- Political pressure lines are active
- Book 5 escalation is inevitable but untriggered
