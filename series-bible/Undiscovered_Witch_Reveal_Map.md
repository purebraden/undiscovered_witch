# UNDISCOVERED WITCH — REVEAL MAP (WHO KNOWS WHAT, WHEN)

## Purpose

This document controls **information flow**.
It prevents premature reveals and protects the slow-burn structure.

---

## Book 1 — Undiscovered

### Allison
- Notices patterns
- Believes they are coincidence
- Does not name power
- Believes calm is good

### Evan
- Knows magic exists (family knowledge)
- Does NOT name it to Allison
- Recognizes anomalies
- Suspects Allison is central

### Kara
- Notices Allison’s distance
- Interprets it socially, not supernaturally

### Emily
- Feels the cost of being “calmed”
- Names emotional loss without explanation

### Veilward Circle
- Not actively aware of Allison
- Alder Creek flagged but inconclusive

### Reader
- Knows something is wrong
- Does not know terminology
- Understands cost before cause

## Book 2 — Inherited

### Allison

- Begins questioning calm

### Evan

- Still not fully inducted

### Circle
- Becomes aware Allison exists
- Begins debate, not action

### Reader
- Learns structure but not full scope
