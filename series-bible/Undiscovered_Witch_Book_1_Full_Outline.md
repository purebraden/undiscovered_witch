# UNDISCOVERED WITCH — BOOK 1 FULL OUTLINE

## Working Subtitle
**Book One: What Doesn’t Settle**

## Core Arc Summary
Book 1 follows Allison Carter as she slowly realizes that her presence alters emotional outcomes. She believes she is keeping people safe by smoothing conflict, until she learns—through loss rather than revelation—that control and care are not the same thing.

The book ends not with answers, but with refusal: Allison chooses not to end something she could.

---

## PROLOGUE — The Adoption
**Purpose:** Establish family dynamics and unease without magic

- Allison’s adoption is framed as loving but distant
- Parents sense something “off” but never name it
- Emily’s biological connection subtly contrasted
- One unsettling line implies Allison has always calmed rooms

**Thematic seeds:** belonging, difference, quiet control

---

## CHAPTER 1 — The Notes App
**Purpose:** Introduce Allison’s internal coping system and school normalcy

- Allison’s position as socially invisible but functional
- Introduction of the Notes app as pattern tracking
- First subtle reality shifts dismissed as coincidence
- Establishment of Kara friendship
- Evan introduced peripherally

**Notes role:** dismissal, minimization

---

## CHAPTER 2 — What Gets Easier
**Purpose:** Show the benefits of Allison’s presence

- Conflicts dissolve around Allison
- Emily names the loss (“I miss being mad”)
- Allison reframes calm as kindness
- First emotional cost is hinted

**Notes role:** defensive rationalization

---

## CHAPTER 3 — Being Seen
**Purpose:** Evan notices Allison differently

- Evan observes patterns Allison ignores
- Quiet connection begins
- Evan hints at knowledge without naming
- Allison experiences being noticed without pressure

**Notes role:** pattern logging without interpretation

---

## CHAPTER 4 — Pressure
**Purpose:** Escalate stakes inside the family

- Emily resists containment
- Allison fails to smooth conflict cleanly
- Family dynamic strains but holds
- Allison mislabels restraint as failure

**Notes role:** first cracks in dismissal

---

## CHAPTER 5 — What Doesn’t Settle
**Purpose:** First conscious non-intervention

- Emily insists on staying mad
- Allison allows conflict to remain
- No praise, no relief
- Allison experiences unresolved tension

**Notes role:** “failure” mislabeling

---

## CHAPTER 6 — What Lingers
**Purpose:** Social consequences of restraint

- Town returns to calm
- Kara notices Allison’s change
- Evan holds back intentionally
- Allison begins to feel visible and wrong

**Notes role:** naming absence

---

## CHAPTER 7 — What It Costs to Stay (MIDPOINT)
**Purpose:** Moral inversion

- Kara is publicly humiliated (bathroom video)
- Allison consciously intervenes
- Conflict stops
- Kara is preserved but hollowed
- Evan witnesses and does not intervene

**Notes role:** first honest entry (“I knew what would happen”)

---

## CHAPTER 8 — What Remains
**Purpose:** Aftershock

- Kara withdraws subtly
- Relationship remains but emptier
- Allison understands the cost of control
- Evan becomes an ethical mirror

**Notes role:** cost and absence

---

## CHAPTER 9 — Ripples
**Purpose:** Consequences widen

- Other students seek Allison out
- Teachers unconsciously rely on her
- Allison feels pressure to perform calm
- Kara keeps distance without confrontation

**Notes role:** containment fatigue

---

## CHAPTER 10 — Resistance
**Purpose:** Allison experiments with refusal

- Allison avoids intervening in escalating situations
- Some conflicts resolve, others worsen
- Allison learns she cannot selectively opt out
- Evan challenges her gently

**Notes role:** observation without control

---

## CHAPTER 11 — The Cost of Silence
**Purpose:** Stakes crystallize

- A situation escalates because Allison does nothing
- Someone is hurt emotionally (not physically)
- Allison realizes refusal also has consequences
- The town labels her “different”

**Notes role:** naming responsibility without justification

---

## CHAPTER 12 — What She Doesn’t End (CLIMAX)
**Purpose:** Final choice of Book 1

- High-pressure emotional conflict
- Allison could end it
- She chooses not to
- The outcome is messy but real
- Evan affirms choice without praise

**Notes role:** refusal to conclude

---

## EPILOGUE — Still Here
**Purpose:** Close the book without resolution

- Kara remains present but changed
- Family intact but imperfect
- The town feels slightly unstable
- Allison accepts uncertainty

**Final Notes Entry (Mirror of Midpoint):**
```
I could have ended it.
I didn’t.

It’s still happening.

I’m still here.
```

---

## Book 1 End State

- Allison no longer believes calm equals good
- Relationships are preserved but honest
- Magic remains unnamed
- The system (Veilward Circle) is still unseen
- The reader understands the moral stakes
