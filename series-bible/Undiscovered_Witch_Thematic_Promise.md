# UNDISCOVERED WITCH — THEMATIC PROMISE DOCUMENT

## Purpose

This document defines what the series **promises the reader emotionally**.

Plot may change.
Themes do not.

---

## Core Themes

### Belonging vs Containment
- Being kept safe is not the same as being wanted
- Belonging requires consent

### Calm as Control
- Quiet is not neutral
- Stability can be coercive

### Agency Over Optimization
- Systems prefer compliance
- Humanity resists efficiency

### Choice Has a Cost
- Freedom is heavier than safety
- Avoidance is still a decision

---

## What This Series Is

- A story about a girl learning she is allowed to want
- A critique of benevolent control
- A slow reclamation of self

---

## What This Series Is NOT

- A power fantasy
- A destiny narrative
- A story where silence equals virtue
- A story where systems know best

---

## Emotional Promise Per Book

- **Book 1:** Unease without explanation  
- **Book 2:** Recognition without safety  
- **Book 3:** Power without permission  
- **Book 4+:** Choice without certainty  

---

## Non-Negotiables

- Allison must choose, not inherit resolution
- No ending where containment is framed as love
- No ending where Allison disappears into silence

---

## Endgame Truth

The story does not end when Allison is powerful.

It ends when she is **allowed to decide who she is**, even if the world is louder because of it.

---

*End of Thematic Promise*
