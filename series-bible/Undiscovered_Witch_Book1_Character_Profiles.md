# UNDISCOVERED WITCH — BOOK 1 CHARACTER PROFILES

## Allison Carter
**Age:** 17  
**Role:** Protagonist  
**Status:** Adopted

### Public Persona
- Quiet, capable, emotionally steady
- Academically successful without visible effort
- Known as “easy,” “mature,” and “reliable”
- Rarely the center of attention, never an outcast

### Internal Landscape
- Deep need to belong without causing disruption
- Equates calm with safety and love
- Suppresses desire to avoid consequences
- Experiences relief rather than pride when problems resolve

### Behavioral Patterns
- Anticipates conflict and intervenes before escalation
- Uses emotional neutrality as self-protection
- Records patterns and coincidences in her Notes app
- Avoids articulating wants

### Book 1 Arc
- Begins believing calm is inherently good
- Gradually notices patterns responding to her presence
- Learns that her “helpfulness” removes agency from others
- Ends Book 1 choosing discomfort over enforced calm

---

## Emily Carter
**Age:** 13  
**Role:** Younger sister  
**Status:** Biological child of the Carters

### Public Persona
- Loud, emotional, expressive
- Seen as dramatic but normal for her age
- Receives more direct attention from parents

### Internal Landscape
- Feels unseen once her emotions are smoothed away
- Wants permission to be angry, upset, and messy
- Begins to resent Allison’s calm interventions

### Behavioral Patterns
- Emotional escalation when not validated
- Increasing resistance to being “fixed”
- Direct confrontation rather than suppression

### Book 1 Arc
- Starts as the child Allison calms
- Becomes the first to question Allison’s role
- Voices the emotional truth Allison avoids
- Serves as the moral anchor of the story

---

## Evan Hale
**Age:** 17–18  
**Role:** New student / Romantic and thematic counterforce

### Public Persona
- Observant, calm but not contained
- Comfortable taking up space
- Emotionally honest, curious
- Seen as “different” but likable

### Hidden Context (Series-Level)
- Comes from a family involved in a secret magical society
- Aware that magic exists, but not formally inducted
- Taught to observe patterns and anomalies without acting

### What He Notices
- Situations that resolve too quickly
- Emotional suppression rather than processing
- That things *end* around Allison instead of resolving

### Book 1 Arc
- Introduces resistance to Allison’s smoothing
- Names emotional truths without naming magic
- Forces Allison to confront wanting
- Ends Book 1 still observing, still choosing not to intervene

---

## Mrs. Halloway
**Age:** Late 60s  
**Role:** Librarian / Quiet observer

### Public Persona
- Kind, observant, deeply rooted in town history
- Trusted by students
- Non-intrusive presence

### Narrative Function
- Notices long-term patterns in Alder Creek
- Serves as a subtle bridge to the town’s past
- Asks questions without demanding answers

### Book 1 Role
- First adult to gently notice anomalies
- Plants unease without revelation

---

## Ms. Raymond
**Age:** 40s  
**Role:** Guidance Counselor

### Public Persona
- Professional, reassuring, efficiency-focused
- Praises stability over ambition

### Narrative Function
- Institutional mirror of containment culture
- Reinforces Allison’s belief that being “fine” is enough

### Book 1 Role
- Validates Allison’s suppression unintentionally
- Demonstrates how systems reward quiet compliance

---

## Mr. and Mrs. Carter
**Role:** Adoptive parents

### Public Persona
- Loving but distant
- Appreciate ease and stability
- Avoid conflict when possible

### Narrative Function
- Reinforce Allison’s role as emotional stabilizer
- Contrast parenting styles between Allison and Emily

### Book 1 Role
- Provide safety without true attunement
- Unaware contributors to Allison’s conditioning

---

## Alder Creek (Setting as Character)
**Type:** Small town

### Characteristics
- Calm, predictable, emotionally regulated
- Conflicts resolve quickly or disappear
- Long stretches of “nothing happening”

### Narrative Function
- Amplifies Allison’s effects
- Reflects the cost of enforced calm
- Serves as a living system responding to suppression

### Book 1 Role
- Begins as comfort
- Slowly becomes unsettling
- Ends as something Allison questions

---

## Notes App (Conceptual Character)
**Type:** Internal narrative device

### Function
- Allison’s attempt to externalize control
- Tracks patterns, coincidences, and rules
- Reveals internal beliefs more honestly than dialogue

### Book 1 Arc
- Starts as observation
- Becomes suppression manual
- Ends as self-interrogation
