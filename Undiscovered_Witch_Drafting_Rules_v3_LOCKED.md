# Undiscovered Witch — Series Drafting Rules v3 (LOCKED)

These rules govern all drafting, revision, and exports for the **Undiscovered Witch** series unless explicitly overridden.

They are **series-level rules** and apply across projects, chats, and books.

---

## RULESET 0 — SERIES AUTHORITY

- These rules are series-level, not project-level  
- New projects must opt in explicitly  
- Rules persist unless versioned  
- Refer to this document as **Drafting Rules v3**

---

## RULESET 1 — SCENE-FIRST WORKFLOW (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

For every scene:

1. Draft **one complete scene only**
2. Post the full scene in chat
3. STOP
4. Wait for explicit approval
5. After approval, provide a **markdown download file**
6. Only then proceed to the next scene

Batching, summarizing future scenes, or drafting ahead is not allowed.

---

## RULESET 2 — SCENE LABELING & FILE FORMAT

- Every scene must use **Chapter.Scene numbering** (e.g., 13.1)
- Beat numbers are invisible scaffolding only

### Scene Export Format

Each approved scene must be exported as a markdown file containing:

# Chapter 13.1

- Full scene prose only  
- No beat references  
- No chapter titles yet  

One file = one scene.

---

## RULESET 2.1 — BEATS ARE INVISIBLE

- Beat numbers may never appear in prose artifacts  
- Beat sheets are planning tools only  

---

## RULESET 3 — SCENE TRANSITIONS (STRICT)

Each scene must transition from the previous scene via exactly one:

- Continuation  
- Consequence  
- Reframing  

If a transition cannot be named, the scene is invalid.

---

## RULESET 3.1 — SCENE LOAD REQUIREMENT

Every scene must carry **at least two** of the following:

- Plot movement  
- Character relationship change  
- World / magic / cultural texture  
- Emotional realization or reframing  

Single-function scenes must be expanded before approval.

---

## RULESET 4 — CHAPTER ASSEMBLY

After all scenes in a chapter are approved:

1. Combine scene prose into one markdown file  
2. Remove all scene headers  
3. Add a single chapter header:

# Chapter 13 — Chapter Title

4. Finalize the chapter title  
5. Preserve prose order only  

One file = one chapter.

---

## RULESET 4.1 — ACT POSITION CHECKPOINTS

At the start of each act, declare:

- Act purpose  
- Target book percentage  
- What must *not* happen yet  

---

## RULESET 5 — ACT ASSEMBLY

After all chapters in an act are assembled:

1. Create one markdown file containing:
   - All chapters in order
   - Chapter headers and titles
   - Full prose
2. No scene headers remain

Example filename:

Book4_ActIV_FULL.md

---

## RULESET 5.1 — EXPORT COMPLETENESS

A drafting phase is incomplete until:

- All scenes are exported  
- All chapters are assembled  
- The act file is created  

---

## RULESET 6 — ESCALATION & MODE CONTROL

- No premature power escalation  
- No unresolved political conclusions  

### Mode Declaration

Each act must declare its narrative mode:
- Containment  
- Recognition  
- Permission  
- Exploration  
- Consequence  

Scenes violating the declared mode must be revised.

---

## RULESET 7 — CANON & REVISION BOUNDARIES

- Approved scenes are soft-locked  
- Chapters supersede scenes  
- Acts supersede chapters  
- Locked acts are immutable unless a revision pass is declared  

---

## RULESET 9 — CANON HIERARCHY

Canon priority (highest to lowest):

1. Locked act files  
2. Assembled chapter files  
3. Approved scene files  
4. Series canon documents  
5. Beat sheets  
6. Unapproved draft output  

Higher levels override lower automatically.

---

## RULESET 10 — META REFERENCES

- Meta references must serve **character awareness**
- Reader-only recap is not allowed  
- Repetition must add new meaning  

---

## RULESET 11 — CANON RECONTEXTUALIZATION

Allowed:
- New meaning for past events  
- Revealed motivations  

Not allowed:
- Retcons  
- Fact contradictions  

Test:
Does this add meaning without changing what happened?

---

## RULESET 12 — INFORMATION REPEAT LIMITS

- No concept may be fully re-explained more than once per book  
- Subsequent mentions must deepen or complicate  

---

## RULESET 13 — VIEWPOINT AUTHORITY

- Information is limited by who is present  
- Institutions do not speak omnisciently  
- Fairies speak from worldview, not system truth  

---

## RULESET 14 — READER ORIENTATION

Orientation must occur through:
- Character reaction  
- Consequence  

Not through recap or exposition blocks.

---

## FINAL NOTE

These rules exist to **protect momentum, wonder, and coherence**.

They may evolve, but only through versioned updates.
