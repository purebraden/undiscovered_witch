# Undiscovered Witch — Book 1 Beat Sheet
**Working Title:** *What Doesn’t Settle*  
**Series:** Undiscovered Witch  
**Genre:** YA Contemporary Fantasy  
**Core Theme:** Belonging vs control; attention as responsibility

---

## ACT I — UNEASE (Chapters 1–4)
**Theme:** Something is wrong, but no one will name it.

### Beat 1 — The Baseline (Chapter 1)
- Establish Allison’s internal world and quiet isolation.
- Family dynamic shows love without attunement.
- Kara exists as proximity, not intimacy.
- First deniable “coincidences.”
- Notes app introduced as coping.
- **Turn:** Allison realizes attention feels like responsibility.

### Beat 2 — Pattern Recognition (Chapter 2)
- Allison notices repetition.
- Begins forming internal “rules.”
- Control brings relief, not joy.
- Notes entries increase and subtly organize.
- **Turn:** Carefulness feels like safety.

### Beat 3 — Social Pressure (Chapter 3)
- School dynamics test restraint.
- Allison intervenes unintentionally.
- Outcome is positive; emotional cost is negative.
- First deletion or suppression of Notes.
- **Turn:** Doing good doesn’t feel good.

### Beat 4 — The Cost of Silence (Chapter 4)
- Near-confession with family that fails by omission.
- Emily contrasts Allison’s restraint.
- Escalation in coincidence scale.
- **Turn:** Belonging seems to require silence.

---

## ACT II — PRESSURE (Chapters 5–9)
**Theme:** Control feels like safety until it isn’t.

### Beat 5 — External Stakes (Chapter 5)
- Allison’s influence affects multiple people.
- Social success, internal unease.
- Notes app shifts from diary to documentation.
- **Turn:** This isn’t just about her anymore.

### Beat 6 — Fracture Without Fallout (Chapter 6)
- Kara experiences distress.
- Allison intervenes indirectly.
- Relationship shifts but survives.
- **Turn:** Safety and closeness diverge.

### MIDPOINT — Loss of Innocence (Chapter 7)
- Public humiliation / cyber incident.
- Allison suppresses escalation successfully.
- Evan observes for the first time.
- **Truth:** Not choosing is still choosing.

### Beat 7 — Being Seen (Chapter 8)
- Evan enters Allison’s orbit quietly.
- He notices what others don’t.
- Allison feels exposed, not validated.
- **Turn:** What if she’s not alone in knowing?

### Beat 8 — Failed Confession (Chapter 9)
- Allison attempts to confide in her mother.
- Receives reassurance instead of recognition.
- Emotional withdrawal follows.
- **Turn:** Even love can miss you.

---

## ACT III — CHOICE (Chapters 10–14 + Epilogue)
**Theme:** Belonging chosen is different than belonging granted.

### Beat 9 — Escalation (Chapter 10)
- Consequences grow harder to manage.
- Control begins reshaping Allison’s identity.
- Subtle understanding moment with Evan.
- **Turn:** Control is costing her selfhood.

### Beat 10 — Breakdown Without Collapse (Chapter 11)
- Allison fails to stop an outcome.
- No catastrophe, but loss matters.
- Notes reflect uncertainty.
- **Turn:** Perfection isn’t possible.

### Beat 11 — Refusal (Chapter 12)
- Allison chooses not to intervene.
- Messy but human outcome.
- First true act of agency.
- **Turn:** She is not responsible for stabilizing everything.

### Beat 12 — Reclaiming Self (Chapter 13)
- Allison allows herself to want something openly.
- Relationship dynamics soften.
- Notes show integration.
- **Turn:** Belonging doesn’t require permission.

### Beat 13 — Integration (Chapter 14)
- Allison lives with ambiguity.
- Magic remains unnamed.
- She stops hiding from herself.
- **Resolution:** Acceptance without answers.

---

## EPILOGUE — The Wider World (Evan POV)
- Evan speaks to his parents.
- Defends Allison’s right not to be explained.
- Veilward Circle implied, not revealed.
- **Series Promise:** Some people belong to themselves.

---

## SERIES PROMISES LOCKED
- No explicit magic naming in Book 1
- Notes app evolves with Allison
- Power = attention, not force
- Belonging is the emotional core
