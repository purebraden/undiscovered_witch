# Undiscovered Witch — Book 1 Structural Map (Hybrid Model)

**Target Length:** 70,000–80,000 words  
**Model:** Hybrid (Anchor + Bridge Chapters)

This map defines which chapters carry structural weight (Anchors) and which intentionally remain lighter (Bridges).  
Anchor chapters are designed to contain multiple scenes, escalation, and fallout.  
Bridge chapters control pacing, tension, and breath.

---

## Chapter-by-Chapter Structure

### Chapter 1 — **Anchor**
**Target:** 4,500–5,000 words  
**Purpose:**  
- Establish Allison’s internal isolation  
- Introduce Notes app as coping mechanism  
- Seed sense of difference without explanation  
- Define baseline family dynamic and emotional distance  

---

### Chapter 2 — **Bridge**
**Target:** 2,000–2,500 words  
**Purpose:**  
- Introduce early “wishes” as coincidences  
- Normal high school life  
- Reinforce Allison’s self-doubt and rationalization  

---

### Chapter 3 — **Anchor**
**Target:** 4,500–5,000 words  
**Purpose:**  
- Moral fracture (Rachel incident)  
- Allison chooses non-intervention  
- First clear cost of inaction  
- Notes shift from observation → guilt  

---

### Chapter 4 — **Bridge**
**Target:** 1,500–2,000 words  
**Purpose:**  
- Introduce Evan as presence  
- Increase pressure without consequence  
- Establish attention as threat  
- Creek becomes emotionally unstable space  

---

### Chapter 5 — **Bridge (Heavy)**
**Target:** 2,000–2,500 words  
**Purpose:**  
- Evan crosses into Allison’s private routine  
- First consensual breach of solitude  
- Creek becomes socially compromised  
- Desire vs fear tension introduced  

---

### Chapter 6 — **Anchor**
**Target:** 4,500–5,000 words  
**Purpose:**  
- Allison tests rules deliberately  
- External consequence occurs without wishing  
- Proof that neutrality does not prevent harm  
- Anxiety escalates from internal → situational  

---

### Chapter 7 — **Bridge**
**Target:** 2,000–2,500 words  
**Purpose:**  
- Aftermath processing  
- Kara relationship tension increases  
- Allison begins active avoidance  

---

### Chapter 8 — **Anchor**
**Target:** 4,500–5,000 words  
**Purpose:**  
- Kara-centered escalation  
- Social fallout and humiliation  
- Allison’s silence causes relational damage  
- Notes reflect shame and fear of being known  

---

### Chapter 9 — **Bridge**
**Target:** 1,800–2,300 words  
**Purpose:**  
- Evan notices pattern of withdrawal  
- Subtle confrontation without revelation  
- Allison’s defenses harden  

---

### Chapter 10 — **Bridge**
**Target:** 2,000–2,500 words  
**Purpose:**  
- School + family pressure  
- Small cracks in parental relationship  
- Allison considers telling someone — doesn’t  

---

### Chapter 11 — **Anchor**
**Target:** 4,500–5,000 words  
**Purpose:**  
- Attempted conversation with mother  
- Emotional invalidation  
- Reinforces core wound: belonging without control  
- Allison pulls inward instead of reaching out  

---

### Chapter 12 — **Bridge**
**Target:** 1,800–2,300 words  
**Purpose:**  
- Escalation without explanation  
- Systemic feeling: things happening *around* Allison  
- Rising dread  

---

### Chapter 13 — **Bridge**
**Target:** 2,000–2,500 words  
**Purpose:**  
- Pre-collapse quiet  
- Evan senses something wrong but doesn’t name it  
- Allison reaches breaking point internally  

---

### Chapter 14 — **Anchor**
**Target:** 5,000–5,500 words  
**Purpose:**  
- Allison realizes neutrality is a choice  
- Internal collapse  
- Choice to stop pretending nothing is happening  
- Sets stage for revelation (not yet delivered)  

---

### Epilogue — **Bridge**
**Target:** 1,200–1,500 words  
**Purpose:**  
- Evan mentions Allison to his parents  
- Introduce larger world awareness  
- Confirm Allison as a variable, not a pawn

- **Anchors:** 6 chapters  
- **Bridges:** 8 chapters + Epilogue  
- **Estimated Total:** ~72,000–78,000 words  

This structure balances:
- Emotional intimacy  
- Narrative momentum  
- Market-appropriate length
