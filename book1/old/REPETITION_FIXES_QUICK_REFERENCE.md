- [Use metaphor instead]

### Example transformations:
- "like she was laying something carefully on a shelf" 
  → "the careful placement of something on a shelf"
  
- "like she was watching other people's lives"
  → "as if observing someone else's life through glass"

## Sentence Rhythm Variations

### Instead of three short sentences:
- "She didn't. She couldn't. She wouldn't."
  → "She didn't—couldn't—wouldn't."
  → "She couldn't do it, wouldn't do it, didn't even want to try."

### Instead of "Not X. Not Y. Just Z.":
- "It wasn't X or Y, just Z."
- "Not X, not Y—just Z, plain and simple."
- "Z, not X or Y."

## Notes App Entries

The lowercase, stream-of-consciousness style is intentional. However, watch for:
- Repetitive sentence structures within entries
- Overuse of "i don't know" / "i don't think" / "i feel like"
- Similar patterns in each entry

Consider varying:
- Sentence length within entries
- The way questions are posed
- The rhythm of the thoughts
