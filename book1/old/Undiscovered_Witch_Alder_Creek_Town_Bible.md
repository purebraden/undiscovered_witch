# UNDISCOVERED WITCH — ALDER CREEK TOWN BIBLE (BOOK 1)

## Overview

**Town Name:** Alder Creek  
**Type:** Small rural/suburban town  
**Population:** ~4,500  
**Vibe:** Calm, familiar, emotionally regulated  
**Reputation:** “Nice place to grow up” / “Nothing ever happens here”

Alder Creek is the kind of town people forget to leave and outsiders struggle to fully understand. It is not eerie on the surface. It is *comfortable*. That comfort is the problem.

The town functions as a living system that subtly rewards emotional suppression, predictability, and stability.

---

## Thematic Function

Alder Creek exists to:
- Reinforce Allison’s belief that calm equals safety
- Normalize emotional suppression
- Make absence feel like resolution
- Blur the line between coincidence and intention

The town responds not to spells or rituals, but to **emotional pressure**.

---

## Geography & Layout

Alder Creek is small enough that most places are reachable on foot or by bike.

### Key Features
- A central main street
- Residential neighborhoods radiating outward
- A creek dividing the town into east and west
- Wooded outskirts that feel less regulated

The further from the center, the weaker the “calm” feels.

---

## Major Locations

### 1. Alder Creek High School
**Function:** Social pressure cooker

- Small graduating classes
- Teachers know students by name
- Conflicts are quickly de-escalated
- Administration values order and safety over disruption

Narrative Role:
- Where Allison’s patterns are most visible
- Where Evan notices resistance
- Where “normal” behavior is most clearly defined

---

### 2. Middle School
**Function:** Emotional volatility zone

- Louder, messier than the high school
- Less effectively smoothed
- Emily’s emotional expression stands out here

Narrative Role:
- Shows the cost of emotional suppression
- Highlights generational differences in control

---

### 3. The Creek & Footbridge
**Function:** Liminal space

- Runs through the heart of town
- The only place Allison consistently pauses
- Emotional pressure feels lighter here

Narrative Role:
- Allison’s grounding space
- Evan’s first real confirmation something is wrong
- Boundary between control and freedom

---

### 4. Alder Creek Public Library
**Function:** Memory keeper

- Old building with newer renovations
- Houses local archives and newspapers
- Quiet without being oppressive

Narrative Role:
- Mrs. Halloway’s domain
- Where town history quietly contradicts the present
- Foreshadows cycles of calm

---

### 5. Main Street
**Function:** Social theater

- Grocery store, café, post office, pharmacy
- Familiar faces everywhere
- Gossip travels politely

Narrative Role:
- Reinforces “everyone knows everyone” dynamic
- Shows how conflict disappears socially

---

### 6. Allison’s House
**Function:** Emotional containment zone

- Comfortable, lived-in, unremarkable
- Arguments resolve quickly
- Silence settles unnaturally fast

Narrative Role:
- Mirrors Allison’s internal role
- Primary site of Emily’s resistance

---

### 7. The Outskirts / Woods
**Function:** Pressure release

- Less maintained roads
- Fewer houses
- Sounds travel differently

Narrative Role:
- Magic weakens here (unnamed in Book 1)
- Allison feels more herself
- Future site of revelations

---

## Social Dynamics

### Conflict Handling
- Raised voices are discouraged
- Emotional displays are subtly redirected
- Resolution is prioritized over processing

### Gossip
- Polite, indirect, non-confrontational
- Scandals fade quickly
- People “move on” without closure

### Authority Structures
- Teachers, counselors, and officials value stability
- Systems reward compliance and balance
- Disruption is framed as immaturity

---

## Weather & Environment

- Weather is unusually cooperative
- Storms delay or soften
- Forecasts change often

Narrative Use:
- Externalizes Allison’s internal state
- Signals loss of control when weather misbehaves

---

## History (Spoiler-Safe)

- Alder Creek has had long stretches of calm decades
- Periods of sudden population shifts
- Records are incomplete but not missing
- Some families have been present “forever”

Mrs. Halloway knows more than she says.

---

## Book 1 Arc for Alder Creek

- Begins as comfort
- Gradually becomes unsettling
- Ends as a system Allison questions

The town does not turn hostile in Book 1.

It simply becomes visible.

---

## Future Use (Series-Level Notes)

- Alder Creek is not unique
- It is a stability pocket
- Others exist
- Some failed

Allison is not the first.
She is the first who stayed.
