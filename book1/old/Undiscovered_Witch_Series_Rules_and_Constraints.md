# UNDISCOVERED WITCH — SERIES RULES & CONSTRAINTS BIBLE

## Purpose of This Document

This document defines **hard boundaries** for the series.
These rules exist to protect tension, prevent power creep, and preserve thematic integrity.

If a future idea violates a rule in this file, the rule wins.

---

## Core Principle

**Power does not create freedom. Choice does.**

Any use of power that removes choice must have a cost.

---

## Rules About Allison

### 1. Allison Cannot Solve Problems Cleanly
- Conflicts may stop, but they do not resolve emotionally
- Stopping escalation is not the same as healing
- Any “solution” leaves residue

### 2. Allison Cannot Control Outcomes Precisely
- She cannot choose *how* things end
- She can only influence whether things escalate or quiet
- Precision equals danger

### 3. Allison Cannot Use Power Without Emotional Cost
- Suppressing desire suppresses self
- Wanting makes pressure louder
- Avoidance is not neutral

### 4. Allison Cannot Be Fully Aware Early
- Understanding lags behind effect
- Language always comes after consequence

---

## Rules About Magic (Unnamed in Book 1)

### 5. Magic Is Not a Tool
- It cannot be “used” on command
- It responds to emotional pressure, not intent
- It resists optimization

### 6. Magic Does Not Fix Systems
- Institutions remain flawed
- People remain responsible for themselves
- Large-scale change always destabilizes something else

### 7. Power Always Leaves Evidence
- Emotional gaps
- Memory inconsistencies
- Environmental shifts

Even when no one notices consciously.

---

## Rules About the Veilward Circle

### 8. The Circle Never Acts Quickly
- Observation is always prioritized
- Delay is considered wisdom

### 9. The Circle Believes Silence Is Kindness
- They equate ignorance with safety
- They mistake containment for care

### 10. The Circle Is Wrong, Not Evil
- Their harm comes from certainty
- They will never see themselves as villains

---

## Escalation Constraints

### 11. No Sudden Power Jumps
- Each increase in capability requires loss elsewhere
- Emotional cost scales faster than ability

### 12. No Clean Victories
- Wins create new problems
- Calm is never free

---

## Absolute No-Gos

- No time travel resets
- No mind control framed as benevolent
- No prophecy that removes agency
- No “chosen one” immunity from consequence
