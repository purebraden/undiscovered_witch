# Undiscovered Witch — Notes App Style Rule

## Purpose
Allison’s Notes app entries are **in-world artifacts**, not standard narration.
They represent private, unfiltered thought and must always be visually distinct from prose.

This distinction helps readers immediately recognize:
- documented thought vs lived experience
- internal processing vs external action
- emotional evolution across the series

---

## Core Formatting Rule

All Notes app entries must be formatted as **indented blocks** with a visible digital cue.

### Required Elements
- Each line begins with a vertical bar `|`
- Block is indented left and right in final layout
- Natural word wrapping (no manual line breaks mid-sentence)
- Single-spaced inside the block
- One blank line before and after the Notes block

### Example
```
| Allison’s Notes — Untitled
| 
| okay this is dumb but i need somewhere to put this
| 
| i don’t think i’m broken
| i just think i notice things
```

---

## Voice Rules (Evolution Over Time)

### Book 1 — Early Allison
- Diary-like
- Emotional, rambling, self-conscious
- Lowercase common
- Direct self-address (“okay this is stupid but…”)
- Shame and fear present

### Mid-Series
- Mixed case
- More structured thoughts
- Early categorization (“rules,” “patterns”)
- Emotional distancing begins

### Later Series
- Formalized headers
- Lists, rules, logs
- Minimal emotional language
- Notes act as self-surveillance

---

## What to Avoid
- No quotation marks
- No italics for Notes blocks
- No font changes inside body text
- No centering
- No poetic forced line breaks

Notes should feel **private and intentional**, not stylized for effect.

---

## Narrative Principle
The Notes app evolves **with Allison**.
Its formatting reflects her changing relationship with:
- control
- responsibility
- belonging
- power

If the Notes stop feeling necessary, the story is broken.
