# Undiscovered Witch — Book 1 Beat Sheet

This document tracks the **narrative beats and emotional escalation** across Book 1, independent of chapter length.
Magic is *never named* in Book 1. All effects remain ambiguous, deniable, and psychological.

---

## Prologue — Before Allison
- Establish adoptive family dynamic
- Subtle sense of wrongness / distance
- Allison’s need to belong seeded
- One unsettling line hinting at inheritance or loss

---

## Chapter 1 — Allison Observes Herself
- High school normalcy
- Allison feels different but can’t articulate why
- Notes app introduced as a way to “prove” she’s not imagining things
- Early internal shame/fear tone
- Kara introduced as friendly but not intimate
- Theme: *Being surrounded ≠ belonging*

---

## Chapter 2 — Coincidences
- Normal teenage wishes (not being called on, not being paired up, etc.)
- Events align *too conveniently*
- Allison rationalizes outcomes
- No intentional wishing
- Lunch with Kara remains normal
- Notes reflect doubt, not belief

---

## Chapter 3 — The First Moral Failure (Rachel)
- Public embarrassment of Rachel
- Allison notices the moment when intervention could help
- She does nothing
- Outcome worsens
- Guilt replaces confusion
- Notes shift from observation → responsibility

---

## Chapter 4 — Attention
- Evan introduced as a presence
- He notices Allison without acting
- Allison becomes hyper-aware of being seen
- Creek introduced as emotional refuge
- Fear of attention overtakes fear of difference

---

## Chapter 5 — Breach
- Evan initiates contact
- Hallway interaction
- After-school walk to the creek
- Creek becomes shared space
- Desire vs fear tension introduced
- Notes reflect loss of control

---

## Chapter 6 — Rules Fail
- Allison creates explicit rules
- She deliberately avoids wishing
- External consequence still occurs
- Proof that neutrality is not protection
- Anxiety escalates from internal → situational

---

## Chapter 7 — Avoidance
- Allison pulls back socially
- Kara notices distance
- Evan senses withdrawal
- Allison attempts to shrink her presence

---

## Chapter 8 — Kara
- Kara experiences public humiliation
- Allison hesitates, then fails to intervene
- Relationship damage occurs
- Shame eclipses guilt
- Notes reflect fear of being known

---

## Chapter 9 — Pattern Recognition
- Evan notices Allison’s behavioral patterns
- Gentle confrontation without accusation
- Allison deflects
- Tension deepens

---

## Chapter 10 — Cracks at Home
- Family pressure increases
- Allison considers telling her mother something
- She doesn’t
- Reinforces emotional isolation

---

## Chapter 11 — Rejection
- Allison attempts to open up to her mother
- Fails to receive comfort or validation
- Core wound reinforced: acceptance is conditional
- Allison turns inward

---

## Chapter 12 — Escalation Without Control
- Things happen *around* Allison
- She is no longer the initiator
- Dread replaces fear

---

## Chapter 13 — Stillness Before Collapse
- Quiet chapter
- Allison reaches emotional breaking point
- Evan senses something is wrong but doesn’t name it

---

## Chapter 14 — Choice
- Allison realizes neutrality is a choice
- She accepts that inaction causes harm
- Decides to stop pretending nothing is happening
- Sets stage for revelation (not yet delivered)

---

## Epilogue — Elsewhere
- Evan speaks to his parents
- Mentions Allison without naming details
- Introduce larger world awareness
- Confirm Allison as a variable, not a pawn

---

## Core Thematic Spine
- Belonging vs control
- Attention as threat
- Choice has cost
- Silence is not neutral
- Identity discovered, not granted
