# Demon Intervention Rules (Locked)

These rules govern how the bound demon intervenes in Rachel’s actions and the world’s response. They are hard constraints, not suggestions.

---

## Rule 1: Reduce Exposure, Not Danger

The demon intervenes to reduce **exposure**, not to protect Rachel from harm.

It will intervene when:
- Rachel approaches crowds
- Rachel becomes memorable or identifiable
- Contact risks classification escalation
- Witnesses are created or sustained

It will not intervene when:
- Rachel is injured
- Rachel is exhausted
- Rachel is frightened
- Rachel is morally distressed

Pain is acceptable. Exposure is not.

---

## Rule 2: Preemptive Intervention

The demon acts **before** Rachel consciously decides.

- Paths are shut down before she understands them
- Muscles lock, nausea spikes, or pain hits without warning
- Rachel experiences correction, not choice

Rachel never gets to attempt the wrong action—only feel the refusal.

---

## Rule 3: Risk-Based Scaling

Intervention scales by **risk**, not morality.

High-risk triggers:
- Children
- Crowds
- Sustained proximity
- Public spaces

Lower-risk triggers:
- Armed threats
- Single pursuers
- Direct violence

Civilians are more dangerous than enemies.

---

## Rule 4: Blunt Force Only

The demon cannot perform precise actions.

It cannot:
- Select individuals cleanly
- Act delicately
- Solve problems surgically

It can:
- Warp space
- Lock movement
- Destabilize environments
- Redirect motion violently

Collateral damage is unavoidable.

---

## Rule 5: Adaptive Pressure

The demon learns Rachel’s resistance points over time.

It tracks:
- Emotional hesitation
- Moral resistance
- Areas of psychological strain

Intervention begins earlier in those spaces as the story progresses.

Effects include:
- Shortened reaction windows
- Memory slips
- Emotional flattening around resisted actions

---

## Rule 6: No Lies, Extreme Omission

If the demon communicates:
- It does not lie
- It answers only what is asked
- It never provides context voluntarily

Most communication occurs via pressure, pain, or refusal.

Speech is rare and significant.

---

## Rule 7: Protect the Binding

The demon prioritizes preservation of the **binding**, not Rachel.

It will allow:
- Injury
- Isolation
- Loss of allies
- Reputation destruction

If the binding is threatened:
- It escalates instantly
- It overrides all other considerations
- It risks annihilation

---

## North Star Check

If the demon’s action makes Rachel safer but more isolated, the intervention is correct.
