# Demon Intervention Rules (Locked)

These rules govern how the bound demon intervenes in Rachel's actions and the world's response. They are hard constraints, not suggestions.

---

## Rule 1: Reduce Exposure, Not Danger

The demon intervenes to reduce **exposure**, not to protect Rachel from harm.

It will intervene when:
- Rachel approaches crowds
- Rachel becomes memorable or identifiable
- Contact risks classification escalation
- Witnesses are created or sustained

It will not intervene when:
- Rachel is injured
- Rachel is exhausted
- Rachel is frightened
- Rachel is morally distressed

Pain is acceptable. Exposure is not.

---

## Rule 2: Preemptive Intervention

The demon acts **before** Rachel consciously decides.

- Paths are shut down before she understands them
- Muscles lock, nausea spikes, or pain hits without warning
- Rachel experiences correction, not choice

Rachel never gets to attempt the wrong action—only feel the refusal.

---

## Rule 3: Risk-Based Scaling

Intervention scales by **risk**, not morality.

High-risk triggers:
- Children
- Crowds
- Sustained proximity
- Public spaces

Lower-risk triggers:
- Armed threats
- Single pursuers
- Direct violence

Civilians are more dangerous than enemies.

---

## Rule 4: Blunt Force Only

The demon cannot perform precise actions.

It cannot:
- Select individuals cleanly
- Act delicately
- Solve problems surgically

It can:
- Warp space
- Lock movement
- Destabilize environments
- Redirect motion violently

Collateral damage is unavoidable.

---

## Rule 5: Adaptive Pressure

The demon learns Rachel's resistance points over time.

It tracks:
- Emotional hesitation
- Moral resistance
- Areas of psychological strain

Intervention begins earlier in those spaces as the story progresses.

Effects include:
- Shortened reaction windows
- Memory slips
- Emotional flattening around resisted actions

---

## Rule 6: No Lies, Extreme Omission

If the demon communicates:
- It does not lie
- It answers only what is asked
- It never provides context voluntarily

Most communication occurs via pressure, pain, or refusal.

Speech is rare and significant.

---

## Rule 7: Protect the Binding

The demon prioritizes preservation of the **binding**, not Rachel.

It will allow:
- Injury
- Isolation
- Loss of allies
- Reputation destruction

If the binding is threatened:
- It escalates instantly
- It overrides all other considerations
- It risks annihilation

---

## North Star Check

If the demon's action makes Rachel safer but more isolated, the intervention is correct.

---

# Part 5 Rule Evolution

The rules above apply through **Chapter 82**. Beginning in **Chapter 83** (completion of the binding), the relationship between Rachel and the demon fundamentally changes.

---

## Pre-Completion (Chapters 76-82): Cooperative Phase

During Part 5 before completion, the demon's behavior shifts:

### Rule 1 Modified: Shared Intent
- The demon still reduces exposure, but now **responds to Rachel's agreement**
- When Rachel consciously aligns with the demon's priorities, interventions feel like cooperation rather than control
- Rachel can now suppress the light by "thinking what the demon thinks"

### Rule 2 Modified: Suggestion Over Correction
- The demon begins **suggesting** rather than forcing
- Rachel experiences guidance, not refusal
- She can override suggestions, but the demon communicates consequences first

### Rule 5 Modified: Mutual Vulnerability
- The demon reveals its own fears and history
- Emotional sharing goes both directions
- The adaptive pressure becomes adaptive **communication**

### Rule 6 Modified: Voluntary Context
- The demon begins offering context without being asked
- Still doesn't lie, but now explains why
- Speech becomes more frequent as integration approaches

---

## Post-Completion (Chapters 83-85): Integration Phase

After the binding completes, the demon is no longer a separate entity. The rules transform:

### New Rule: No More Interventions
- The demon cannot "intervene" because there is no separation
- Rachel's instincts **are** the demon's priorities
- What felt like external correction now feels like internal preference

### New Rule: Merged Priorities
- Exposure still feels dangerous—but as Rachel's own anxiety, not imposed pressure
- Crowds still feel wrong—but as her own discomfort, not forced avoidance
- The demon's caution has become Rachel's intuition

### New Rule: Unified Action
- Powers respond to Rachel's intent directly
- No more negotiation between host and passenger
- What Rachel wants, she does—but what she wants has changed

### New Rule: Borrowed Instincts
- Rachel notices preferences that weren't hers before
- She avoids things the demon avoided, without knowing why
- Some of her "choices" are the demon's habits wearing her shape

---

## North Star Check (Post-Completion)

If Rachel's instinct feels foreign but protective, the integration is working correctly.

If she can't tell whether a thought is hers or the demon's, the integration is complete.
