# Chapter 68 — The Convocation

Rachel didn’t ask for everything at once. The demons answered what they were asked, not what she wanted, and she was learning the difference.

She asked about the circle. About the people who’d built it. About why it had been there at all.

“They perform controlled summonings,” the presence said. “Bindings placed into vessels they own. Servants. Tools. The circle you fell through was meant to make one.”

Rachel thought of the young summoner with shaking hands, the dead anchor half in the ring. “And it broke.”

“Yes,” it said. “It made something else.”

An uncontrolled binding. Demon and human fused wrong. The phrase settled into place as she pieced together the fragments.

“They’ve been trying to recapture me,” she said.

“To extract what they placed,” the presence replied. “To rebind it properly.”

The pressure in her chest stirred, dry and faintly offended.

*They think I’m a tool,* the voice said. *They’re not entirely wrong.*

Rachel tilted her head. “What do you think you are.”

The pause that followed was longer than most. Not hesitation. Measurement.

*Incomplete,* the voice said.

Rachel felt the word settle and stay. Incomplete meant there had been a shape it was supposed to finish in. A place it was supposed to land.

She found herself wondering what a complete demon would look like.
