# Chapter 72 — Telling Lena

Rachel found Lena where the room liked her best, seated in a shallow curve of the floor, the air around her calm and settled. The resistance between them felt thicker now, or maybe Rachel was just paying better attention.

“I’m leaving,” Rachel said.

Lena nodded slowly. She’d been expecting the words. That didn’t make them easier.

“There’s a place I have to go,” Rachel continued. “It’s where they wanted the demon to end up. I’m going to finish what they started—just not the way they wanted.”

Lena’s hands curled. “And if it goes wrong.”

“Then I die and you never see me again.”

“And if it goes right.”

Rachel hesitated. “Then I’m… different. Finished. And I still probably never see you again.”

Lena exhaled, a laugh that didn’t carry any humor. “That’s a shit deal either way.”

“I know.”

They sat in the quiet for a moment, the room holding them apart as gently as it could.

Lena reached out. The resistance pushed back. She pushed through anyway, fingertips brushing Rachel’s wrist, a touch so light it could have been imaginary and still felt like a promise.

“Go,” Lena said. “Find out what you’re for. Then come back and tell me.”

Rachel swallowed. “Will you be okay.”

Lena’s mouth tilted. “I’m not alone,” she said. “I’m held.”

Rachel nodded once. The pressure in her chest eased, just a fraction.
