# Chapter 70 — The Original Destination

Rachel waited until Lena was asleep before asking again. The pressure in her chest didn’t mind sleep. It never had.

“Where was it supposed to go,” she asked.

The answer came slower this time, as if the route to it was longer.

*It was being transferred,* the voice said. *From a holding space. A prison. Something like this, but worse.*

“And the destination.”

*A prepared vessel. A Convocation stronghold. They call it the Relay.*

Rachel felt the word settle. Relay. A place built to receive something that couldn’t move on its own.

“If I go there,” she said, “they take it.”

*If you go there, they try,* the voice replied. *You can let them. Or you can finish it yourself.*

“And if I finish it.”

*Then we’re fused. Permanent. Impossible to separate.*

The pressure in her chest shifted, not approval, not fear. Need.

Rachel stared into the dim. “Do you want to go there.”

*I want to be finished,* the voice said. *One way or another.*

The answer sat between her ribs and didn’t move.

For the first time, she understood that the thing inside her wasn’t just dangerous.

It was trapped.
