# Chapter 71 — Not a Tool

Rachel found a corner where the hum thinned and sat with her back to a wall that wasn’t quite solid. The pressure in her chest waited. It always did.

“You’ve been steering me this whole time,” she said. “Keeping me hidden. Not for my sake.”

*No,* the voice said. *For the binding.*

“But you kept me alive.”

*You are the binding. If you die wrong, it breaks wrong.*

Rachel closed her eyes. “And if I go to the Relay. If I finish it.”

The pause that followed wasn’t empty. It was weight.

*Then you choose what we become. No one else.*

The words landed like a door unlatched. Rachel sat with them and felt the shape of the thing inside her shift — not softer, not kinder. Just clear.

The demon didn’t want freedom. It wanted to stop being passed around. It wanted to be placed. Not in a holding space. In something that chose it.

Rachel opened her eyes and stared at the floor until the seams stopped trying to tell her what to do.

“I’ll go,” she said.

The pressure stirred, attentive.

“But not for you,” she added. “For me.”

The voice didn’t answer. It didn’t need to.
