# Chapter 65 — The Binding’s Purpose

Night here didn’t arrive so much as settle. The light thinned, the low hum dropped a note, and people drifted into positions that felt less like sleep and more like maintenance.

Lena lay on her side with her hands tucked beneath her chin, breathing even. The room held her without asking. Rachel sat a few feet away, knees up, back against a low rise that wasn’t quite a wall.

The pressure in her chest stayed awake.

“Why won’t they kill me,” she asked, keeping her voice low.

There was a pause, short and precise.

*Because you’re carrying something they want.*

Rachel’s throat tightened. “What.”

*Me.*

She stared at the floor until the seams stopped trying to mean something. “Why did they summon you.”

*Movement,* the voice said.

“That’s not—”

*Demons do not move easily. Humans do.*

The words sat in her ribs and stayed there. A demon placed into a body so it could be moved, carried, deployed. A vessel built with muscle instead of ritual.

Rachel pressed her hand to her sternum. The pressure met it from the inside, steady and unyielding. “So I’m a vehicle.”

*You are a binding,* the voice said. *Vehicles break. Bindings hold.*

She swallowed. “Why me.”

*The circle broke. You were nearby. You held.*

Rachel closed her eyes. The memory of the room, the blood, the light, slid behind her eyelids and stayed there, bright and wrong. “What were you supposed to do.”

Silence.

She waited, breath shallow, the room dim around her, Lena’s breathing steady and out of reach.

“What were you supposed to do,” she asked again.

The pressure shifted, not away, not closer. Unreadable.

No answer came.

Rachel stayed where she was, the question hanging between her ribs like something unfinished.
