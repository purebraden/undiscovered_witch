# Chapter 69 — What She Could Do

Rachel asked the demons what her choices were.

The question hung for a beat and then unfolded in a list that felt too clean for the room it belonged to.

“One,” the presence said. “Stay here. Safe. Held. Purposeless.”

The pressure in her chest barely moved. Indifferent.

“Two. Let the Convocation take you. The binding breaks. You die.”

The pressure spiked, sharp and immediate, like a hand yanking away from heat.

“Three. Go to where the binding was meant to finish. Complete it on your terms.”

The pressure didn’t answer at all. It waited.

“Four. Destroy the binding entirely. You and it both end.”

Silence. Cold and flat.

Lena had been listening from the side. “You’re not seriously considering any of these,” she said.

Rachel met her eyes. “I’m considering all of them.”

Lena shook her head, jaw tight. “That’s not the same as choosing.”

Rachel looked back at the presence. “What does completing it mean.”

The room didn’t answer yet.

The pressure in her chest tightened, attentive.

Rachel felt the question settle into her, heavy and specific. She knew she wasn’t going to get anything else until she followed it.
