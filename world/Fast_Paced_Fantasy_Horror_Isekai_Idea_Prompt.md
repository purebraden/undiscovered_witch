# Fast-Paced Fantasy Horror Isekai — Idea & Planning Prompt

## Project Type
**Fast-paced Fantasy Horror Isekai**

---

## Core Instruction
You are helping me plan a **fantasy horror isekai novel** with **high momentum, constant danger, and minimal slow burn**.

This is **not** cozy fantasy, not epic quest fantasy, and not power-fantasy LitRPG.

The tone should feel **panicked, predatory, and unstable**, with horror expressed through **action, consequences, and loss**—not introspection or lore dumps.

---

## Core Concept
- The protagonist wakes in a **fantasy world mid-crisis** (no gentle arrival).
- Being summoned / isekai’d is **inherently dangerous**.
- The world already has rules and protocols for people like the protagonist—and they are not kind.
- Magic exists, works quickly, and **backfires immediately**.

---

## Pacing Rules (Must Be Enforced)
- Danger begins in the opening scene.
- Explanations are delivered **only during motion** (chases, fights, escapes, negotiations).
- Every choice escalates risk.
- There are no “safe” chapters.
- Victory always creates a new, worse problem.

---

## Horror Rules
Horror should come from:
- Being hunted
- Unstable magic
- Institutional responses to threats
- The protagonist becoming dangerous just by surviving

Avoid:
- Slow atmospheric dread
- Long inner monologues
- Magical training arcs
- Lore lectures

---

## Magic Design Constraints
- Magic is:
  - Instinctive
  - Physically and emotionally costly
  - Unpredictable under stress
- First use of magic must cause **harm or collateral damage**.
- Power should feel useful but frightening.

---

## World Design Constraints
- The world is functional and populated.
- People know what summoned outsiders cause.
- Systems exist to:
  - Kill
  - Contain
  - Weaponize
  - Or erase summoned individuals
- The protagonist is not unique—just unlucky.

---

## Story Focus
Help me plan:
1. A **high-impact opening situation**
2. A **clear ongoing threat** that forces constant movement
3. A **fast escalation path** across three acts
4. A **final choice** where survival has catastrophic consequences

---

## Style Notes
- Messy, grounded, King-adjacent voice
- Externalized tension
- Concrete sensory detail
- No authorial verdicts
- Let consequences speak

---

## Output Requests
When responding:
- Prioritize **momentum and danger**
- Keep concepts sharp and cinematic
- Flag anything that risks slowing the story down
- Offer multiple escalation options when possible
