# Drafting Rules for Parts 1-4

Use this file when drafting chapters for Parts 1-4.

**For Part 5, use `Part5_Drafting_Prompt.md` instead.**

Do not rewrite or repeat existing content. Continue from the last line of the previous part.

## Continuity Anchor
- Part 1 ends with a containment unit locking onto Rachel in a dark chamber.
- Rachel is injured (leg, shoulder), exhausted, and bound to a demon.
- The institution treats her as a containment breach, not a person.
- Lena is gone and off-page, not safe.

## Chapter Format
- Use Markdown headers: "# Chapter 17", "# Chapter 18", etc.
- Keep chapter length consistent with Part 1: 1,800-2,600 words.
- Each chapter must have a clear goal, obstacle, escalation, and consequence.
- End on a hook that forces the next move.

## Tone and Style
- Messy, grounded, King-adjacent voice.
- Externalized tension. Concrete sensory detail.
- No long inner monologues. No lore dumps.
- Explanations only during motion.

## Pacing Rules
- Danger begins immediately in every chapter.
- No safe chapters.
- Every choice escalates risk.
- Victory creates a worse problem.

## Isekai Requirements (every chapter)
- Show at least one world detail that is not Earth-normal:
  - sky, weather, materials, architecture, sounds, technology, biology
  - institutional protocols that are clearly non-Earth
  - language mismatch or translation bleed
- Rachel must have at least one "what the hell" moment per chapter.

## Demon Rules (hard constraints)
- The demon reduces exposure, not danger.
- It intervenes before Rachel fully decides.
- It cannot act delicately. Blunt force only. Collateral is normal.
- It does not lie. It answers only what is asked.
- Communication is rare and significant:
  - allow at most one short word or phrase in a chapter
  - prefer pressure, nausea, refusal, or cold attention over dialogue

## Institutional Response Rules
- The world has protocols for outsiders.
- Staff respond as if this has happened before.
- Protocols may conflict between departments. Show friction.

## Avoid
- Training arcs.
- Power-fantasy leveling.
- Cozy interludes.
- Explanatory info-dumps without motion.

## Must Keep
- Rachel is not unique, just unlucky.
- The binding is dangerous and isolating.
- The institution is more afraid of exposure than violence.
